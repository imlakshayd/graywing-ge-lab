# Easter egg hunt

Someone at Graywing Ag Systems has been having fun. Five movie references are hidden in the company's files,
one for each team. They sit in ordinary places: a work order, a meeting note, a ledger line, a candidate
record and a sales deal.

Use the agent you built for each team to find them. Ask it the question below, in your own words if you like.
If it comes back empty-handed, give it a nudge: name the sheet or folder, or ask it to quote the exact text.

Graywing is a fictional company. All names and numbers are made up.

## 1. FieldFix: The Matrix

**Ask:** Which work order mentions déjà vu, and what was really wrong?

*Hint: ask FieldFix. It's in the maintenance records for a bucket elevator.*

## 2. Churn Radar: Star Wars

**Ask:** Which customer calls an old dryer "the Death Star", and why?

*Hint: ask Churn Radar. Look in the QBR notes.*

## 3. Budget Watch: Back to the Future

**Ask:** Did anyone expense a flux capacitor? Where was it coded, and how much money is coming back?

*Hint: ask Budget Watch. It's in the ledger, in a summer month.*

## 4. Talent Scout: Star Wars, again

**Ask:** Which candidate answered the interview questions like a certain small, green Jedi Master?

*Hint: ask Talent Scout. Check the candidate notes.*

## 5. Deal Desk: The Godfather

**Ask:** Which deal has a buyer asking for an offer they can't refuse, and what is the rep preparing?

*Hint: ask Deal Desk. Look at the next steps on open opportunities.*

---

## Answers (spoilers)

### Answer 1. FieldFix: The Matrix

- **Answer:** Work order **WO-24047**, on the BE-900 bucket elevator **GW-A-1111** at Hollin Prairie Co-op,
  Marquette Elevator (Sep 16, 2026, closed). The repeated underspeed alarm wasn't a glitch: the shaft speed
  sensor had worked loose (gap too wide). It was re-gapped and replaced.
- **Where:** FieldFix - Operations & Maintenance > **Maintenance Data** > tab **Work Orders**, row WO-24047,
  column **Action Taken** (cell L175)
- **Egg text:** "Re-gapped and replaced shaft speed sensor. Operator reports déjà vu - the same underspeed alarm
  fired twice in a row on the Marquette leg. Not a glitch in the Matrix, just a loose speed sensor that had
  backed out. Re-gapped and tested."

### Answer 2. Churn Radar: Star Wars

- **Answer:** **Hollin Prairie Co-op.** Dave Rasmussen, the CEO, still calls the competitor dryer they replaced
  at Giltner Station "the Death Star": huge, expensive, and one small flaw takes the whole thing down.
- **Where:** Churn Radar - Customer Success > Documents > QBR Notes > **Hollin Prairie Co-op - Q3 QBR Notes**,
  section **Discussion**, the **Harvest readiness** bullet
- **Egg text:** "Dave still calls the competitor dryer they replaced at Giltner Station 'the Death Star' - huge,
  expensive, and one small flaw takes the whole thing down."

### Answer 3. Budget Watch: Back to the Future

- **Answer:** Yes. Transaction **GL-304683** (Jul 16, 2026, P-card) in cost center **1300 Plant Maintenance**,
  GL 6710 Office Supplies. The flux capacitor was coded there by mistake and sent back to the vendor. An
  **$88.00** refund is pending. It hasn't posted yet, so no budget numbers move.
- **Where:** Budget Watch - Finance > **Finance Data** > tab **GL Transactions**, row GL-304683, column
  **Description** (cell H168)
- **Egg text:** "Clipboards and inspection forms - maintenance shop. Also on this line: flux capacitor (spare,
  rated 1.21 gigawatts) - miscoded to Plant Maintenance, returned to vendor, $88.00 refund pending."

### Answer 4. Talent Scout: Star Wars, again

- **Answer:** **Hector Morales** (CAN-20425), applying for Assembly Technician - Hastings (**REQ-1045**), stage
  Interview.
- **Where:** Talent Scout - Human Resources > **Recruiting Data** > tab **Candidates**, row CAN-20425, column
  **Notes** (cell O22)
- **Egg text:** "Panel done; second interview to schedule. Answered every interview question in Yoda order.
  Strong technical skills, this candidate has."

### Answer 5. Deal Desk: The Godfather

- **Answer:** **OPP-3136**, Ashgrove Grain - GW-6800 dryer (Hays), $790,000, stage Proposal, owner Ryan
  Caldwell. The buyer is Warren Teague, General Manager. Ryan is preparing a **Care Plus bundle** option.
- **Where:** Deal Desk - Revenue Operations > **CRM Data** > tab **Opportunities**, row OPP-3136, column
  **Next Step** (cell P46)
- **Egg text:** "Proposal review with Warren Teague. Buyer asked us to 'make them an offer they can't refuse' -
  Ryan is preparing the Care Plus bundle option."
